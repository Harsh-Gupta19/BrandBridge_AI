What changed, and why

Ten revisions and twelve new screens, each traceable to a requirement in the build manual. Frame numbers below refer to this package.

Revised from v1
The Wall kept, and given a clear job  frames 10, 11
It is a peer community — brands and creators sharing ideas, open calls and results, and following each other. Two rules make it safe: wall content never feeds the ranking model, and a thread converts into a normal proposal rather than a side channel.
Home became an activity digest  frames 09, 15
Separate from the Wall: assembled from campaigns, matches and proposals you already store, so the two surfaces do different work.
One percentage → two numbers  frames 12, 14, 18, 20
A single “94% mutual fit” contradicted the principle that recommendations expose components, not a score.
Freshness and provenance on every metric  frames 07, 18, 20, 29
“84K followers” without a date reads as current truth.
Eligibility made inspectable  frames 18, 19
Rule accuracy and false-exclusion rate are reported metrics, so the decisions have to be visible.
Bars carry numerals and bands  frames 18, 20
Rate card visible while quoting · agreement clauses expand to source  frames 14, 24
New in v2
The whole creator journey  frames 06–13
Nine of eleven v1 frames were brand mode. Half of a two-sided product was undrawn: profile, rate card, preferences, connected accounts, opportunity feed, campaign detail.
Proposals in depth  frames 21, 22, 23
Creator drafting with the evidence the draft used and a guideline check; a role-aware inbox; and a version-by-version term comparison that flags when a counter breaks the campaign budget cap.
Knowledge in depth  frames 26, 27, 28
The library with per-document scope and retirement, the five-stage ingestion pipeline, a chunk inspector for debugging answers, and the creator-side view where answers pin to a proposal and out-of-scope questions are refused.
